## Algorithm for boiling rice

## Inputs
- Rice
- Water
- Cooking Pot
- Stove
- Salt

## Process 

START
1. Measure out the amount of rice needed
2. Rinse the rise
3. Add enough water to a cooking pot
4. Put the rice into the cooking pot
5. Add salt 
6. Place the pot on a stove
7. Turn on the stove
8. Wait till the rice starts to cook
9. Check whether the rice is soft
10. If the rice is not yet soft, add a small amount of water and continue cooking
11. If the rice is soft, turn off the stove

END

## Output
Cooking rice by boiling
